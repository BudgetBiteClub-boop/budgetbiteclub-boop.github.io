Budget Spicy Rigatoni alla Vodka

$4.94

The $34 restaurant spicy rigatoni... for $4.94 a pot. That's $1.24 a plate, and honestly? Budget wins.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb rigatoni
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1/2 yellow onion, finely diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1/4 cup tomato paste
  • 1 tsp red pepper flakes
  • 2 tbsp vodka
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 1/2 cup grated parmesan
  • Salt
  • 1/2 cup reserved pasta water

Steps

  1. Salt your water like the sea and drop one pound of rigatoni. Pull it one minute early.
  2. Meanwhile, two tablespoons butter, half a diced onion, two cloves of garlic. Soft and sweet, not brown.
  3. Quarter cup of tomato paste. Fry it till brick red, because caramelized sugars are the whole restaurant secret.
  4. One teaspoon chili flakes, then two tablespoons of vodka. Hear that hiss? The alcohol cooks off and wakes up the tomato.
  5. Half a cup of heavy cream. Stir and watch it go blush pink and silky.
  6. Rigatoni in with a splash of starchy pasta water. That starch glues sauce to every ridge.
  7. Half a cup of parmesan, off the heat so it melts instead of clumping. Wait for that gloss.
