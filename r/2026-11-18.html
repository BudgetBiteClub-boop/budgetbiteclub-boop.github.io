Takeout-Style Leftover Turkey Fried Rice

About $4.80* · 2 servings · 20 min

*Prices are estimates and may differ from what you pay. How we price recipes

Delivery fried rice: $18 and 45 minutes. Mine: $4.80 and 20 minutes, with leftover turkey.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup (200 g) long-grain white rice
  • 1 1/4 cups (300 ml) water plus a pinch of salt
  • 2 tbsp (30 ml) vegetable oil, divided
  • 1/2 medium onion (100 g), diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup (120 g) frozen mixed vegetables (peas, carrots, corn)
  • 1 cup (120 g) leftover roast turkey, shredded bite-size (refrigerated no more than 3-4 days)
  • 2 large eggs, beaten with a pinch of salt
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1/8 tsp white or black pepper

Steps

  1. Rinse the rice until the water runs mostly clear. Add it to a small pot with 300 ml water and a pinch of salt, then bring to a boil over high heat. Cover, turn the heat to low and simmer for 12 minutes. Take it off the heat, rest it covered for 2 minutes, then spread it on a large plate or sheet pan to steam off while you prep.
  2. While the rice cooks, dice the onion, mince the garlic, shred the turkey into bite-size pieces and beat the eggs with a pinch of salt.
  3. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large skillet or wok over high heat until it shimmers. Cook the onion for 2 minutes. Add the garlic for 30 seconds, then add the frozen vegetables and stir-fry for 2 minutes until hot and any water has cooked off.
  4. Push the vegetables to one side and add the remaining 1 tbsp oil to the empty side. Pour in the eggs and scramble for 30-45 seconds until just set, then break them up into the vegetables.
  5. Add the rice and turkey. Press everything flat, let it sit for 1 minute to toast, then toss. Repeat for 3-4 minutes until the rice crackles and the turkey is steaming hot all the way through (74°C/165°F).
  6. Drizzle the soy sauce around the hot edge of the pan, add the pepper and toss for 30 seconds. Taste, adjust the salt and serve immediately.
