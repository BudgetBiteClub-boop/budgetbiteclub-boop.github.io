Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Budget Bite Club ("we", "us") runs a small private tool, the Budget Bite Club Uploader, that publishes our own cooking videos to our own accounts on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram. This policy explains what that tool accesses and how it is handled.

Who uses the tool

The tool is used only by the owner of Budget Bite Club to post to Budget Bite Club's own accounts. It is not offered to the public and does not sign in other users.

What data the tool accesses

  • YouTube (Google): the tool requests the youtube.upload permission, which lets it upload videos to our channel. It does not read, store or share any other Google account data, viewer data or analytics.
  • TikTok: the tool uses TikTok's Content Posting API to upload our videos to our own TikTok account.
  • Instagram (Meta): the tool uses the Instagram Graph API to publish Reels to our own Instagram account.

How data is stored

Access tokens issued by these platforms are stored only on the owner's computer and are used solely to upload our videos. We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Viewers of our videos

We do not collect personal information from people who watch our videos. Each platform handles viewer data under its own privacy policy.

Google and YouTube

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke the tool's access at any time from your Google account security settings.

Deleting data

Revoking access in your Google, TikTok or Meta account settings immediately stops the tool from posting. Stored tokens can be deleted on request.
