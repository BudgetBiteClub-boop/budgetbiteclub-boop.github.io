Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Budget Bite Club ("we", "us") runs a small private tool, the Budget Bite Club Uploader, that publishes our own cooking videos to our own accounts: YouTube @Budget_Bite_Club, TikTok @budgetbiteclub and Instagram @budget_biteclub. This policy explains what that tool accesses and how it is handled.

Who uses the tool

The tool is used only by the owner of Budget Bite Club to post to Budget Bite Club's own accounts. It is not offered to the public and does not sign in other users.

What data the tool accesses

  • YouTube (Google): the tool requests the youtube.upload permission, which lets it upload videos to our channel. It does not read, store or share any other Google account data, viewer data or analytics.
  • TikTok: the tool uses TikTok's Content Posting API to upload our videos to our own TikTok account.
  • Instagram (Meta): the tool uses the Instagram Graph API to publish Reels to our own Instagram account.

How data is stored

Access tokens issued by these platforms are stored only on the owner's computer and are used solely to upload our videos. We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Viewers of our videos

We do not collect personal information from people who watch our videos. Each platform handles viewer data under its own privacy policy.

The $5 Recipe Builder, Meal Planner and your account

  • What you type (ingredients, likes, allergies) is sent to our server and to Anthropic's Claude AI to write your recipe or meal plan. Don't type personal details into these boxes.
  • On your device: your recent recipes, last meal plan, pantry and chosen store are kept in your own browser, and stay there until you clear your browser data.
  • Sign in with Google (optional): if you sign in, Google tells us your first name and email address, and we save your pantry, last meal plan and chosen store on our server (Cloudflare) so they appear on any device. We use your email only to show who's signed in. We never post anything to your Google account, and we don't see your password. You can delete your saved data at any time with "Delete my saved data" while signed in.
  • "Email me my list": the address you enter is used to send that one email (and to add you to the newsletter only if you tick that box). We don't keep a list of these addresses.
  • Counts, not people: we count how many recipes are made and how many shopping lists are sent to a store, and which stores are picked, without recording who did it. Cloudflare Web Analytics counts visits without cookies.

Affiliate links

Some recipe pages and video descriptions include "What you'll need" links to Amazon.ca. Budget Bite Club is a participant in the Amazon Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program; as an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases, at no extra cost to you. When you click one of these links, Amazon may use cookies to record the referral under Amazon's privacy notice. We don't receive your name, order details or payment information.

Newsletter

If you sign up for our newsletter, your email address is stored by our email provider, Kit, and used only to send the newsletter. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can ask us to delete your address at any time.

Google and YouTube

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke the tool's access at any time from your Google account security settings.

Deleting data

Revoking access in your Google, TikTok or Meta account settings immediately stops the tool from posting. Stored tokens can be deleted on request. If you signed in to the Recipe Builder or Meal Planner, "Delete my saved data" removes your account and everything saved with it, or email us and we'll delete it for you.
